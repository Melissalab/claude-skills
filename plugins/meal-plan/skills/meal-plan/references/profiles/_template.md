# Profile Template

Copy this file and rename it for each new person. Fill in every section before using the profile to build a meal plan. If information is missing or uncertain, note that explicitly rather than leaving it blank, because the meal plan quality depends directly on the accuracy of these inputs.

## Lifestyle and activity

Occupation: (What do they do all day? Desk work, physical labor, driving, standing?)

Living situation: (Do they cook for one, for a family, or for a roommate? Do they do their own shopping and prep?)

Background and communication style: (Anything about how they communicate or what tone lands well with them? Do they prefer direct advice, gentle encouragement, practical instructions, or something else?)

Activity multiplier for TDEE calculation: (1.2 sedentary, 1.375 light, 1.55 moderate, 1.725 very active, 1.9 extreme)

## Goals and health context

Primary goal: (Maintain weight, lose weight, gain weight, manage a specific condition, general health?)

Medical context: (Any conditions that affect diet planning? Diabetes, high blood pressure, kidney issues, food sensitivities, pregnancy, recent surgery?)

Currently working with a doctor or specialist? (Yes or no, and who if relevant)

Current medications that interact with food or appetite?

## Dietary preferences and restrictions

Foods they enjoy: (Be specific. "Likes beef" is weaker than "likes ribeye, ground beef in tacos, and cold sliced roast beef but dislikes stew meat")

Strong preferences: (Hot versus cold lunches, specific time budgets, any ingredients they love so much they want them in most meals)

Allergies: (Nuts, shellfish, dairy, gluten, eggs, soy, others)

Dislikes or dealbreakers: (Foods they will not eat regardless of how it is prepared)

Religious or ethical restrictions: (Vegetarian, vegan, kosher, halal, etc.)

## Equipment and shopping

Cooking equipment available: (Air fryer, stovetop, oven, slow cooker, instant pot, grill, microwave, etc.)

Primary grocery source: (Kroger, Whole Foods, Amazon Fresh, Costco, local grocery, etc.)

Secondary or backup grocery source:

Time budget: (Per week for shopping, per meal for cooking, per day for prep)

## Notes from previous plans

(If this person has had plans built before, note what worked, what did not, any adjustments made mid-week, and anything they mentioned about how they felt on the plan. This section grows over time and makes future plans more accurate.)
